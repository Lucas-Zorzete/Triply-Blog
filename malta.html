Malta: The Mediterranean Jewel - 7-Day Diary

Malta was a magical mix of ancient history, stunning blue waters, and incredible food. Here's a look into our 7-day adventure across the islands of Malta and Gozo.

Day 1 - Valletta: A Fortress of History

Our journey began in **Valletta**, the capital, where we checked into the Grand Hotel Excelsior. We spent the morning exploring the magnificent **St. John's Co-Cathedral**. The sheer grandeur and the Caravaggio painting *The Beheading of Saint John the Baptist* left us speechless. For lunch, we tried our first *Pastizzi*, a savory flaky pastry filled with either ricotta cheese or mushy peas. It was the perfect street food! Later, we watched the sunset from the Upper Barrakka Gardens, overlooking the Grand Harbour. It was the perfect start to our trip.

Day 2 - The Three Cities: Timeless Charm

We took a ferry to the **Three Cities** (Vittoriosa, Senglea, and Cospicua). These ancient fortified cities offered a quieter glimpse into Maltese life. We wandered through the narrow, winding alleys, or *karrozzi*, of Vittoriosa. We had an authentic dinner at a small local restaurant, savoring *Fenek* (traditional Maltese rabbit stew), which was surprisingly tender and rich in flavor. It was a day where we truly felt like we stepped back in time.

Day 3 - Mdina & Rabat: The Silent City's Secrets

Today was all about **Mdina**, the 'Silent City'. The medieval walls and Baroque architecture were breathtakingly preserved. We visited the **St. Paul's Cathedral** and simply enjoyed the quiet atmosphere. Just outside the walls, in **Rabat**, we explored the **St. Paul's Catacombs**, an extensive network of underground tombs. It was a fascinating, if slightly eerie, experience. We stopped for *Imqaret* (a deep-fried date pastry) at a local café for a sweet afternoon treat.

Day 4 - Blue Grotto & Marsaxlokk: Azure Wonders

We started early to visit the **Blue Grotto**. We took a small boat tour, and the water inside the caves was an unbelievable azure blue—truly one of the most beautiful natural sights we had ever seen. In the afternoon, we drove to **Marsaxlokk**, a charming fishing village famous for its colorful traditional *luzzu* boats. We had a spectacular fresh seafood lunch by the water, savoring the daily catch.

Day 5 - Gozo: A Smaller, Greener Sister

We took the ferry to **Gozo Island**. It was a much greener and calmer island. We visited the **Ġgantija Temples**, one of the oldest free-standing structures in the world, predating Stonehenge! We also explored the **Cittadella** in Victoria, Gozo’s capital, which offered panoramic views. We stayed overnight at the Kempinski Hotel San Lawrenz, treating ourselves to some luxury after a long day of sightseeing.

Day 6 - Comino: The Crystal Lagoon

Today was pure relaxation. We took a short boat trip from Gozo to **Comino Island** to see the **Blue Lagoon**. The water here was absolutely turquoise and impossibly clear—perfect for swimming. We spent hours snorkeling and soaking up the sun. We bought a pineapple drink from a floating stand and enjoyed the vibrant atmosphere. In the late afternoon, we returned to Malta, feeling completely refreshed.

Day 7 - Golden Bay and Farewell

On our final morning, we headed to **Golden Bay**, one of Malta’s best sandy beaches, for a last swim in the Mediterranean. We tried *Kannoli*—a sweet ricotta-filled pastry—for breakfast before heading to the airport. Malta’s mix of history, sun, and sea was unforgettable. We were sad to leave, but already planning our next "Triply" adventure!
