Ireland: Emerald Isles and Timeless Pubs - 7-Day Diary

The green fields, the dramatic coastline, and the warm welcome of the people made our trip to Ireland truly unforgettable. Here is the diary of our seven days on the Emerald Isle, from Dublin's energy to the wild west coast.

Day 1 - Dublin: The Black Stuff and Book of Kells

We landed in Dublin and checked into the luxurious The Merrion Hotel. Our first stop was the famous Guinness Storehouse. We learned all about the brewing process and finished with a complimentary pint at the Gravity Bar, which offered 360-degree views of the city. Later, we visited the Trinity College Library to see the stunning Book of Kells. For dinner, we had a traditional Irish Stew in a cozy pub near Temple Bar.

Day 2 - Castles and Traditional Pubs

Today was for history. We explored the Dublin Castle, which served as the seat of the UK government administration in Ireland for centuries. In the afternoon, we ventured into the neighborhood of Kilmainham to visit the Kilmainham Gaol, a deeply moving experience. The evening was spent in the oldest pub in Dublin, The Brazen Head, enjoying some live traditional music and a warm bowl of Boxty (a type of potato pancake).

Day 3 - Westward to Galway

We took the train west to Galway City, the vibrant cultural heart of the west coast. After checking into a lovely B&B, we spent the day walking along the Salthill Promenade and exploring the Latin Quarter, famous for its narrow streets and buskers. We had a fantastic seafood dinner, featuring fresh mussels and clams. Galway’s energy was infectious!

Day 4 - The Dramatic Cliffs of Moher

The highlight of the trip for many, and certainly for us, was visiting the Cliffs of Moher. The sheer scale and drama of the cliffs plunging 700 feet into the Atlantic Ocean were breathtaking. The wind was fierce, but the views were worth it! We also saw O'Brien's Tower O'Brien's Tower at the north end of the Cliffs. We drove back to Galway late, absolutely exhausted but fulfilled.

Day 5 - Connemara's Rugged Beauty

We dedicated the day to exploring **Connemara National Park. The landscape was rugged and stunning, with mountains, bogs, and lakes everywhere. We hiked Diamond Hill and visited the serene Kylemore Abbey, a beautiful Victorian castle and former Benedictine monastery. We stayed the night near Galway at the charming Glenlo Abbey Hotel, enjoying a well-deserved rest.

Day 6 - The Gift of the Gab in Blarney

A long journey south brought us to County Cork. The main event was visiting Blarney Castle. We waited in line to kiss the famous Blarney Stone, hoping to gain the "gift of the gab"! It was a fun and slightly scary experience. In Cork City, we tried Coddle, a rustic Dublin sausage and bacon broth, which was surprisingly comforting on a chilly evening.

Day 7 - Cobh and the Titanic Legacy

Our final day was spent in Cobh (pronounced Cove), a beautiful harbor town that was the last port of call for the Titanic. The Titanic Experience Cobh was poignant and well done. We took a moment to reflect on our amazing week before heading to Cork Airport. Ireland exceeded every expectation—we will definitely be back!
