England: Royalty, Rock, and Roman History - 7-Day Diary

From the bustling streets of London to the ancient countryside, England offered a fascinating journey through history, culture, and, of course, proper English tea. Here is our 7-day diary.

Day 1 - London: Royal Sights and Grandeur

Our trip started in the vibrant capital. We checked into the elegant The Royal Horseguards Hotel. The first stop was Buckingham Palace to witness the Changing of the Guard. In the afternoon, we walked across the Westminster Bridge to get the perfect shot of the Houses of Parliament and Big Ben (officially the Elizabeth Tower). For dinner, we enjoyed a classic Fish and Chips at a traditional London pub.

Day 2 - Tower of London and the Crown Jewels

We dedicated the second day to the dark and fascinating history of the Tower of London. Seeing the Crown Jewels up close was dazzling, and hearing the stories from the Yeoman Warders (Beefeaters) was a highlight. In the evening, we caught a show in the famous West End. Before the show, we had a quick and tasty Sausage Roll from a bakery.

Day 3 - Mystical Stonehenge

Today was a trip out of London to the Salisbury Plain to see the prehistoric monument of Stonehenge. It’s incredible to stand there and ponder how these huge stones were placed thousands of years ago. The atmosphere was truly mystical. We learned about its possible uses as a burial ground or astronomical clock. The drive through the English countryside was beautiful, filled with rolling hills and sheep.

Day 4 - Roman Baths and Georgian Architecture in Bath

We traveled further west to the city of Bath, famous for its Roman heritage and Georgian architecture. We spent the morning exploring the incredibly well-preserved Roman Baths, a complex of ancient public bathing houses that still flow with natural hot water. Later, we admired the beautiful Royal Crescent, a row of 30 terraced houses. We stopped for a proper Afternoon Tea with scones and clotted cream—a truly English experience!

Day 5 - Liverpool: The Home of The Beatles

We took the train north to Liverpool. This city is synonymous with music, particularly The Beatles. We visited The Beatles Story museum on the Albert Dock and walked down the famous Penny Lane. The city was vibrant and full of history. We stayed at the The Titanic Hotel Liverpool, a fantastic conversion of an old warehouse.

Day 6 - Manchester's Industrial Heritage and Football

A quick hop over to Manchester, known as the UK's second city. We toured the Old Trafford stadium (home of Manchester United) and the National Football Museum, diving into the history of the world's most popular sport. Later, we explored the regenerated area around the Canal Street and enjoyed a hearty Sunday Roast Dinner (even though it wasn't Sunday!) with Yorkshire pudding and gravy.

Day 7 - Windsor Castle and Farewell

On our final day, we traveled back south near London to visit Windsor Castle, the oldest and largest occupied castle in the world and one of the King's official residences. We walked through the opulent State Apartments and the beautiful St. George's Chapel. It was a grand finish to a week of history and culture. Before heading to Heathrow, we grabbed a classic Pasty for a quick bite. England truly impressed us with its blend of ancient tradition and modern vibrancy.
